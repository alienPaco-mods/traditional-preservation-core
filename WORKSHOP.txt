[h1]Traditional Preservation (Core)[/h1]

The shared mod of the Traditional Preservation series. Every Traditional Preservation mod (Salt, Fish, Yeast, Vinegar, Dairy) requires it. It holds what they share, and one station of its own: the Hearth.

[b]Build 42 only (42.20 or newer). This is an early release, so back up your save before enabling it. Singleplayer only: multiplayer is not supported.[/b]

[h2]What it adds[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]The Hearth[/b]: a stone fireplace with a 20 L copper set in its top, for heating any fluid
[*]One weather record for the whole series, so every station keeps working while you are away
[*]Three kinds of place a station can stand in: outdoors, under a roof and in a basement
[*]The popups and info windows that show a station's progress, real time left, temperature and working rate
[/list]

[h2]The Hearth[/h2]
Build menu, Masonry. Masonry 2: 12 Clay Bricks, 4 Clay, 1 Copper Sheet and a mason's trowel (kept).

It is a working fireplace: add fuel and light it as you would any other, cook in its firebox, warm the room. Its right-click menu also has:
[list]
[*][b]Pour into the Copper[/b]: up to 20 L of one fluid, from any container.
[*][b]Fill from the Copper[/b]: into your own containers, at the copper's heat.
[*][b]Bank the Fire[/b]: the fire burns a third of the wood, holds the copper at about 50 C and cooks a third as fast. [b]Stoke the Fire[/b] brings it back.
[*][b]Hearth Info[/b] (or just click the Hearth): the fire, the fuel and how long it will burn, the copper's contents and heat, and when it will steam.
[/list]

With the fire lit the copper heats towards 95 C and steams at 85 C: about 1.5 hours for 10 L, about 2 for a full 20 L. With the fire out it cools slowly, as the stone holds its heat. The copper shows empty, full or steaming, and the fire mouth glows.

A Hearth can't be picked up while its fire burns or its copper holds anything.

[h2]Places[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Outdoors[/b]: the day's weather.
[*][b]Under a roof[/b] (any roof overhead, walls or not): the last 10 days' average outdoors plus 10 C, never over 28 C.
[*][b]In a basement[/b]: 15 C all year.
[/list]
Mods that ask for it (Dairy) also count [b]by a fire[/b]: any lit fireplace, antique stove, campfire or Hearth within 2 tiles holds 28 C, and a banked Hearth's embers 24 C.
