[h1]Traditional Preservation (Core)[/h1]

The shared mod of the Traditional Preservation series. Every Traditional Preservation mod (Salt, Fish, Yeast, Vinegar, Dairy) requires it. It holds what they share, and the stations they share: the Stone Hearth, the Forged Cauldron and the Smokehouse.

[b]Build 42 only (42.22 or newer). This is an early release, so back up your save before enabling it. Singleplayer only: multiplayer is not supported.[/b]

[h2]What it adds[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]The Stone Hearth[/b]: a knee-high stone fire with an iron door and a stone-lined hole in its top: an oven, a hob over the hole, and heat for the stations round it
[*][b]The Forged Cauldron[/b]: 60 L of forged iron hung by a chain over the Stone Hearth's hole, for heating any fluid
[*][b]The Smokehouse[/b]: a plank shed two tiles long, joined to a Stone Hearth by a clay pipe or a stone trench, where the TP mods smoke their food
[*]One weather record for the whole series, so every station keeps working while you are away
[*]Eight kinds of place a station can stand in: open air, under an open roof, indoors, a loft, a basement, in a shed, by a fire and by banked embers
[*]The popups and info windows that show a station's progress, real time left, temperature and working rate
[*]A [b]Season[/b] menu for foods the other mods let you season afterwards, as a stew (Fish & Meat's sausages): garlic, onion, pepper or herbs, each a little nicer to eat
[/list]

[h2]The Stone Hearth[/h2]
Build menu, Masonry. Masonry 2: 8 Clay Bricks, 2 Clay and a mason's trowel (kept).

It is a working fireplace: add fuel and light it as you would any other, cook in it, warm the room. The hole shows what is cooking: open with smoke rising, a lidded forged pot for stews and fluids, a steel lid for oven food. Its right-click menu also has [b]Bank the Fire[/b] (a third of the wood, warms only the 2 tiles round it, cooks a third as fast; [b]Stoke the Fire[/b] brings it back) and [b]Stone Hearth Info[/b] (or just click it): the fire, the fuel and how long it will burn.

It can't be picked up while it burns, while the cauldron is fitted or while it is joined to a Smokehouse.

[h2]The Forged Cauldron[/h2]
Forge it at a vanilla forge as the forged cooking pot is made: 2 Steel Sheets, 3 Charcoal, 1 Wire, Blacksmith 4, learned from the magazine that teaches the forged pot (or at Blacksmith 5 with Cooking 2). Then [b]Fit Cauldron[/b] from the Stone Hearth's menu with 2 Iron Pipes, a Heavy Chain and a Large Hook (pliers kept); [b]Remove Cauldron[/b] gives it all back. Fitted, the menu also has:
[list]
[*][b]Pour into the Cauldron[/b]: up to 60 L of one fluid, from any container.
[*][b]Fill from the Cauldron[/b]: into your own containers, at the cauldron's heat.
[*][b]Empty the Cauldron[/b]: pour the whole lot away, rotten milk and all.
[/list]

With the fire lit the cauldron heats towards 95 C and steams at 85 C: about 1.5 hours for 10 L, about 2.75 for a full 60 L. Water alone is let boil: tainted water held at the boil for 10 minutes comes out clean (about 1.75 hours for 10 L, 3.25 for a full cauldron). Banked, it holds about 50 C. With the fire out it cools slowly, as the stone holds its heat. What it holds shows in its own colour, and Stone Hearth Info shows its contents, heat and when it will steam.

[h2]The Smokehouse[/h2]

A plank shed two tiles long under a flat roof, its wide door on one end (Woodwork 3: 14 planks, 20 nails, hammer kept). Open air or under an open roof, never in a room; 6 stacks. Right-click it or a Stone Hearth beside it to [b]Connect the Stone Hearth[/b] with a clay pipe (2 Clay Pipe Segments, 1 Clay, a Mason's Trowel kept) or a stone trench (3 Flat Stones, 2 Clay, a shovel kept). You can still cook in the Stone Hearth and fit the cauldron while it smokes. What hangs inside and what the smoke does to it is each mod's: Fish & Meat smokes fish, meat and sausage there.

The tile in front of the Stone Hearth's and the Smokehouse's doors must stay clear: neither builds or goes down with something in front of it. A picked-up Stone Hearth turns to either facing as you put it down.

[h2]Places[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Open air[/b]: the day's weather.
[*][b]Under an open roof[/b] (a porch, an open shed) and [b]indoors[/b] (a room): the last 10 days' average outdoors, a room 2 C warmer in the cold months. A room is 22 C while the power grid is on.
[*][b]In a loft[/b] (the top storey: a room upstairs with no room over it): as indoors, plus three tenths of whatever the 10-day average is above 10 C: the sun on the roof (about 24 C in a 21 C July, no warmer than a room from October to April); 22 C while the power grid is on.
[*][b]In a basement[/b]: follows the ground, a month behind the season and two fifths as far: about 5 C from January to March, 13 C from July to September.
[*][b]In a shed[/b] (inside the Smokehouse, a closed plank shed out of doors): the day's weather, as the open air, out of the rain and wind; the power grid doesn't warm it.
[/list]
Yeast, Vinegar and Dairy also count [b]by a fire[/b]: any lit fireplace, antique stove, campfire or Stone Hearth within 2 tiles holds 28 C indoors, and a banked Stone Hearth's embers 24 C. Outside, the air carries the heat off: under an open roof a fire adds 15 C to the 10-day average (embers 10), in the open 10 C to the day's weather (embers 5), never past 28 and 24.
